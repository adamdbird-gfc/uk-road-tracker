## Identity decision

The [Trust's Manchester map description](https://shop.walkwheelcycletrust.org.uk/products/merseyside-manchester-cycle-map-25)
names National Route 601 between Oldham and Ashton. The targeted official GIS
capture from 18 October explicitly assigns the reviewed main features to
NCN601 and marks the selected 30 features Open. This supports a correction for
specific mapped source spans, rather than renaming an entire old relation on
the basis of the future orbital scheme. The map edition is May 2021 and is
identity corroboration, not an inspection of current access.

The captured [OSM relation 9300597](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9300597)
retains `network=ncn`, `ref=626` and a UK network anchor. Its original tags are
not edited. Current captured membership, source node chains and complete
refreshed provider geometry were checked before the source-label correction.
The ordinary source builder continues to reject the mismatched target number
without an explicit identity review and its independently supplied checksum.

The final review pins the original source relation bytes and versions, source
snapshot hashes, provider payload, complete target feature payload, exactly
30 approved open feature identities and the selected source vertex spans.
Only those source edges can pass assessment. Every existing node, provider,
membership, branch, access and 10 m extent check still applies. All other target
features remain exclusions, including nearby closed features. The review cannot
promote a closure, proposed feature, diversion, alternative or unknown status.

Full source-way coordinates are retained for identity matching. Only the
selected reference spans are eligible geometry; the unselected parts must not
be rendered or credited as part of the open collection.

No whole-route alias is created, old journey evidence is not transferred, and
the preceding zero-association NCN626 inventory is unchanged. The same source
edges are not admitted to a second active NCN626 catalogue. Shared physical
accounting continues to use the original source way/node identities.

## Open-only completion scope

The open-extent manifest preserves all 30 officially open features, with an
explicit `official_open_main_only` basis. Its 6,316.975 m union is independent
of how much can currently be matched to captured source ways. Closed features,
construction and imagined future orbital connections do not add to this scope.
The 81 m NCN626 diversion is not substituted for an NCN601 main section.

Unmatched open geometry remains in the open target. At a 10 m diagnostic
corridor, approximately 177.644 m of official open geometry is outside the
supported source spans. That number locates further review; it does not grant
credit, certify a missing path or define a completion percentage. Disconnected
sections remain disconnected, and no connector is invented between them.

The historical NCN626 source-inventory denominator remains about 6.64 km for
research provenance. It includes captured unsupported source ways and is not
the collection's open-only target. Likewise, the supported source length must
not replace the full official open target to make this route appear complete.

The precise binding between physical source travel and the complete official
open target is still an Android/physical-logical adapter step. Consequently the
open-extent manifest explicitly has `completion_binding_ready=false`. Dividing
the source and official lengths would mix two geometry bases; no user-facing
completion percentage is released by this data assessment. The adapter must
retain unmatched open targets, preserve mode eligibility and count each physical
path once before broader activation.

## Catalogue and validation

The supported set becomes **183 route keys and 43,103 distinct supported source
ways**, adding 25 source ways absent from the preceding supported set. These are
section-catalogue assessment counts, not full-route certifications or travelled
distance. NCN601 moves from missing suitable membership to a reviewed open-span
catalogue with explicit residual holds. The other 65 national source-review keys
remain pending. The legacy NCN626 inventory and four deferred regional
inventories remain unactivated.

The new reader requires an independently pinned manifest, exact reference,
association, identity-review and open-extent bytes. It verifies that the open
feature identities match the review, all open statuses are explicit, only the
approved source spans appear, and the retained denominator equals the complete
projected open geometry union. A reduced denominator is rejected even when the
caller supplies self-consistent replacement file hashes.

Ten regressions cover scope, snapshot/provider/root pins, ordinary mismatch
rejection, restricted source spans, closure masks, unchanged access/geometry
gates, malformed membership spans, reader integrity, retained unmatched open
geometry and zero target credit for travel on an unapproved part of the same
source way. The replay also checks the real construction-way exclusion and that
admitted geometry stays clear of the closed-feature corridor.

All 288 NCN tests and five shared cycling-evidence tests passed, along with
the Route1 and portable-association fixture builds. All 562 original national
inventory files remain byte-identical to their saved baseline.
