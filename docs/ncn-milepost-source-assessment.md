# Geograph milepost evidence and user-supplied route leads

16 October 2026. The user supplied the Geograph NCN article's Route 1–4 milepost
page and 25 Wikipedia named-route leads. These are discovery inputs, not an
approved unlock catalogue or a statement that every full route belongs to NCN.

## Geograph

The article page has 182 tabular entries and 37 MP references. Its table has
locations, grid references, designs and linked photographs. It credits Oast House
Archive and identifies CC BY-SA 2.0; individual photo records credit their own
creators. The licence permits commercial reuse subject to its attribution and
ShareAlike conditions. Preserve a separately attributed source rather than
relabelling it as the official NCN dataset or OGL. Its permissions do not establish
rights to reproduce the verse or photographed artwork itself, or permit reuse of
the separately restricted official milepost inventory.

The linked KML search returned the article's image collection (566 image results),
not a verified list of 566 distinct mileposts. Images can repeat the same object;
camera locations and subject locations are separate fields. A table's four-digit
grid code is a 1km square, not a precise unlock point. Individual records can be
more precise but retain their stated precision and observation date.

Those are candidate correspondences, not verified identities or visit evidence.
The photos are historical and cannot prove current presence/condition. Subject
coordinates are not necessarily a surveyed post position. Disc identity, series
and verse-line association remain unverified. Do not award a mystery achievement
from a design type, MP number, coarse grid square or nearest-point join alone.
No verse text, image asset or official inventory has been added to the app.

## Named-route checklist

The separate assessment’s `user-named-route-checklist.json` preserves all 25 user-supplied
names and Wikipedia links. The initial check found current official-directory
leads for 16 of them (including named variants/sections); this does not establish
current NCN designation, exact geometry, accessibility or a complete inventory.
Two Tunnels Greenway and Way of the Roses already have bounded geometry candidates.
Heron Trail and Darent Valley Path have additional owner sources. Missing directory
matches remain unresolved, not presumed withdrawn.

Examples needing explicit scope:

- Celtic Trail: whole route and East/West variants are separately listed.
- W2W: Walney to Wear and Whitby, with branches/variants; not one invented endpoint.
- Darent Valley Path: the walking route and shorter NCN Route 125 are not assumed
  equivalent. Owner sources also describe an extension, so verify the intended
  route version before assigning endpoints or length.
- North West Trail: the user's Wikipedia link was a red link, but the official
  directory has a lead; missing Wikipedia content is not evidence of no route.
  Cross-border sections must respect UK-only progress scope.

Next: use these leads to recover open mapped membership and verify extents; use
Geograph selectively to corroborate milepost identity and disc observations. Keep
source licences, precision, dates and unresolved questions with every record.
No app/runtime, database or release change is part of this source assessment.
