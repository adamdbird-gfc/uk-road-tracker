94 map approvals, four former-pub exclusions and one temporary-closure hold.
Approvals have an individual current operator listing, checked name/locality,
address/postcode evidence and regular opening schedule. A closed-hours label
alone is not evidence of permanent closure.

## Former pubs and temporary closure

- Grey Friar, Preston: CAMRA branch's July 2026 report confirms Wetherspoon
  departure in June and reopening under independent Friar Limited.
- Cotton Bale, Hyde: CAMRA identifies Amber Taverns, following Wetherspoon
  departure in 2017; same Market Place premises/postcode.
- John Masefield, New Ferry: current CAMRA links it to The Cyclist after sale
  and renaming in 2023; preserve the former source identity.
- Merton, Bootle: current CAMRA identifies a previous Wetherspoon under an
  independent operator at the same Merton Road premises.

Exclude these from current collection membership while preserving historical
identities. Do not equate leaving Wetherspoon with the business closing.
Historical operator pages and old directory entries cannot establish current
membership.

Art Picture House, Bury is explicitly temporarily closed on the current operator
page, with an advertised reopening of 4 December 2026. Hold it from the active
map under the existing schema until actual reopening is verified. It is not a
former-pub exclusion; no automatic activation simply when that date passes.
This case is recorded for explicit temporary-status handling in future updates.
