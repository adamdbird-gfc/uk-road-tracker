## Exclusions and status conflicts

Atrium is excluded: the current CAMRA branch entry records long-term closure
from 6 August 2025, and the operator trading update confirms NEC lease expiry.
Only Piazza is established as the current franchise. Preserve Atrium's old
identity; neither nearby position nor shared postcode permits merging it with
Piazza or granting current membership.

Butler's Bell in Stafford is excluded from current Wetherspoon membership:
its current named operator is Davenports. The pub continues to operate under
that brand; exclusion does not mean business closure. Missing source fields
remain missing.

Court of Requests and Bottle of Sack are held pending reopening verification.
Earlier operator retrievals explicitly show temporary closures and advertised
reopening dates of 10 October and 5 October 2026 respectively. Later cached
retrievals omit those notices and show ordinary closed/open-hours statuses.
The evidence conflicts; actual reopening has not independently been confirmed.
Passed advertised dates alone do not reactivate a held pub. These are not
former-membership exclusions or claims that the pubs are definitely still
closed. Reconciliation records both cases for fresh checks before the full
catalogue release. Ordinary out-of-hours closed labels on other scheduled
operator pages are not treated as permanent or temporary closure.

The independent researcher list is used for corroborating names/localities,
not as an authoritative catalogue. Birmingham Airport is additionally checked
despite omission from that regional list. No further operating pub absent from
the pinned candidates was identified in this cross-check; missing-venue
reconciliation and full-coverage guarantee remain incomplete.
