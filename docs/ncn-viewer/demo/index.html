How to read this view
Select a red dashed edge on the map or its distance bar. Locate selection fits it on screen; 40 m close-up examines its midpoint. The two shaded corridor layers show what “within 10 metres” means in each projection. Use the section selector for tiny end sections and keyboard navigation.
A projection is a way of flattening the Earth to measure distances. The native admission test asks whether the whole source edge stays inside the approved route corridor. Completion correspondence checks clipped portions against a finite target, with direction and extent gates. A passing geometric comparison alone does not establish route membership, access or completion eligibility.
