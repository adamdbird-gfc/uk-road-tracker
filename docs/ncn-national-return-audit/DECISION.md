# NCN remaining assessability: investment decision

5 November 2026 · read-only national audit · issue #265

**STOP — park broad NCN assessability development.** No intervention currently has an evidence-supported forecast of 100 km safely recoverable through a bounded reusable correction. Do not commission another geographical correction programme from this audit.

The deficit is a mixture of highly fragmented residuals and some long connected gaps. It is **not established** that most remaining kilometres arise from a small set of correctable bugs. Nor has this audit proved that most are legitimately excluded or irrecoverable. Historical rejection mechanisms are measurable; underlying route identity, access, edition and admission causes are largely unverified. That uncertainty is a reason to withhold investment and credit, not a forecast of recovery.

- Exact revision 4: **1,288.762517 km** unassessable; **92.608243626372%** assessable.
- Merged, unpublished #276 comparison: **1,286.633901 km** remaining; **92.620452401678%** assessable. Its **2.128616 km** gain is already deducted here.
- **225,706** canonical gap fragments; median **0.543 m**, 99th percentile **77.090 m**. The largest 100 fragments contain only **43.227 km / 3.36%**.
- Joining coincident geometry endpoints produces **128,636** connected components. There are **113 over 1 km**, totalling **294.952 km**; the largest 100 contain **281.435 km / 21.87%**. Connection does not establish a shared technical cause.
- The old proximity queue, now canonically unioned and reduced by #276, covers **611.457 km**. It is not recoverable distance. A conservative admitted-edge / single-option / maximum sampled separation 2 m / existing 15° screen leaves **1.717 km**, still unverified. This screen is neither a recovery lower bound nor an upper bound on all possible corrections.

**Investment recommendation:** prioritise core journey capture, matching reliability and device acceptance. Retain this register for future corroborating evidence. Reopen national assessability only when a concrete generic mechanism comes with exact finite attribution and a national candidate union supporting at least 100 km (or an unusually cheap, low-risk 25–100 km proposal). There is no implementation brief or additional session commissioned here.

**Audit limit:** exact accounting, fragmentation and screen measurement completed; a current national semantic root-cause partition and positive recovery forecasts could not be established from retained evidence. They would require fresh section-level admission/identity/access evidence and missing-source comparison. The bounded audit stops here rather than rebuilding national datasets. “STOP” is a prioritisation decision on available evidence, not a claim that 1,287 km can never be recovered.
