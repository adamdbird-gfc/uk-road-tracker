# Spoons achievements and UI consistency — 0.25.193 (239)

This release implements 21 achievement cards: fourteen named pub visits, one progressive collector family (1/10/25/50/100/250/500 distinct pubs), three venue types, Stage presence, the four home nations and The grand tour. Achievements appear in a Wetherspoons group and jump menu when the collection is installed. Existing accepted visits qualify retrospectively; incorrect-proof removal recalculates progress. No drinks order, GPS proximity alone, route line or raw nearby Timeline place earns a badge. Existing live and historical evidence requirements remain intact.

The grand tour covers Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and nine English regions. Either nominated London pub (Rochester Castle/Sir John Hawkshaw) and either South East pub (Royal Victoria Pavilion/Opera House) satisfies that area. Stage presence is a fixed four-pub curated set: The Wallaw, The Palladium, Opera House and Royal Victoria Pavilion. Each challenge exposes its eligible/remaining venues. Sunday night at the Palladium accepts visits on any day. Square Peg's title is Round Holes, as agreed with the user; no unsupported longest-in-Europe claim is shown.

Named identities are pinned in PubAchievements.NAMED, deliberately distinguishing similarly named pubs (Glasgow Counting House and Bury St Edmunds Corn Exchange). Venue types use a sourced stable-ID registry in collections/wetherspoons-achievement-venues-v1.json: 10 railway station premises, 10 airports and nine Haven parks. Station premises include attached/converted station buildings; a pub on Station Road alone is insufficient. Registry updates require a review; names never automatically classify pubs.

Operator membership/address sources are linked in existing map review ledgers and the new category registry. Stage provenance: https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pub-histories/the-wallaw-blyth/ ; https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pub-histories/the-palladium-llandudno/ ; https://www.jdwetherspoon.com/pub-histories/opera-house-royal-tunbridge-wells/ ; https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1336672 . Haven membership is documented at https://franchising.jdwetherspoon.com/franchises/the-five-stones-primrose-valley-wetherspoon/ .

All 66 empty towns are populated from reviewed operator addresses, with dated town evidence in the map ledger. The map builder validates overrides, preserving the same 811 IDs, coordinates and membership decisions. The bundled/published catalogue revision is 2026101202; update cadence is unchanged. Older accepted proofs receive missing town/nation metadata from the reviewed identity so retained history stays usable after future catalogue removal.

Dialog audit: shared/custom map cards use one gold Close control at the top right; footer Close duplicates are removed. Native information/confirmation dialogs use the same header and navy styling, retaining their callbacks. Journey details put Debug before a separate final Delete Journey row. Pub history removal actions move below primary actions; destructive confirmations put Cancel before the final red delete/remove action. Undoable route-edit toolbar controls remain beside Restore/Undo as editing controls rather than deletion of saved records. Data management already places destructive actions after import and backup.

Location check requests a fresh foreground fix with a 15-second provider duration and 20-second UI timeout. It shows a spinner and disables repeated requests, offers clear retry feedback, and ignores late callbacks after timeout/close. Unsupported fixes report distance and accuracy; valid fixes still require explicit named confirmation and are revalidated when saved. Permission and device-location failures never award visits.

Deferred: Pub crawl, whole-county completion and Last orders require further complete/historical catalogue work. Capture, route matching and update schedule are unchanged. Validation includes achievement identities, alternative regional representatives, nation/stage membership, deduplication, historical proofs, incorrect-proof removal, location timeout/retry/confirmation and dialog callback/layout regressions.
