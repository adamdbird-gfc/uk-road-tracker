## Exclusions and proposals

- New Baron of Hinckley: current CAMRA branch evidence identifies Crafty Pub
  Group's October 2019 opening in former Wetherspoon premises. Source LE10 0AZ
  and CAMRA LE10 0QA differ; retain historical identity without overwriting it.
- Regent, Kirkby-in-Ashfield: CAMRA July 2026 update identifies an independent
  operator after Wetherspoon departure in 2024. Kingsway/Diamond Avenue and
  NG17 7BB/NG17 7GR variants are recorded. The old Wetherspoon history page
  cannot establish current chain membership.
- Court House, Mansfield: Proper Pubs currently lists the Market Place venue
  at NG18 1HX; September 2026 CAMRA evidence corroborates former membership.
- Running Horse, Doncaster Airport: CAMRA branch's Spring 2023 report confirms
  closure with the airport. The October 2026 regional cross-check still lists
  it as former/closed; no current operator pub listing verified. Reopening
  requires fresh evidence and must not activate from airport plans alone.

The first three exclusions concern chain membership, not closure of the
operating pub. An out-of-hours closed label on a scheduled operator page does
not mean permanent closure.

No additional operating pub absent from the pinned snapshot was identified in
the regional names/localities cross-check. The 58 current regional venues are
corroborated individually, including the previously approved Stag and Pheasant.
The researcher list is corroboration only; full-coverage guarantee remains off.

The reported Oast House conversion in Derby is now a reconciliation lead.
An actual operating Wetherspoon pub listing and opening were not established;
